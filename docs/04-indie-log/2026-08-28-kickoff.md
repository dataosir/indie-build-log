# Kickoff — Leaving the Grind, Building for the World

**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Tag:** `#kickoff` `#transition` `#indie` `#ai` `#global`

## Why today

I am no longer optimizing for the next title inside a large org. Unemployment (or the end of a familiar employment track) is not a pause to “wait for the next offer” — it is a forced clarity window.

The old loop was clear: deliver in a local/enterprise context, climb, compete on hours and politics, ship value that rarely compounds for *me*. I am choosing to step out of that 内卷 cycle and treat this period as a deliberate reset: **full-time exploration of AI + globally oriented micro-SaaS / developer tools**, as a one-person company in formation.

## What I am walking away from

- Competing primarily on local ladders, headcount theater, and “busy = valuable”
- Building systems whose upside accrues almost entirely to an employer
- Waiting for permission (roadmap, budget, org chart) before learning or shipping
- Treating English-speaking / global markets as “later,” after domestic comfort

## What I am walking toward

1. **Global market first** — research niches where willingness to pay, distribution, and English content already work; validate before over-building.
2. **AI as leverage, not theater** — use AI to compress research, coding, ops, and writing so one person can cover more surface area.
3. **One-person company habits** — shipping cadence, public accountability, simple metrics, and ruthless scope control.
4. **Developer-adjacent products** — lean on senior Java / backend strength while expanding into tools, automation, and SaaS that other builders will pay for.

## Principles for the next 90 days

- **Ship in public** — write what I learn; prefer imperfect posts over silent perfection.
- **One primary bet at a time** — parallel ideas are allowed in notes; execution stays serial.
- **Global distribution mindset** — English README, English product surface unless research says otherwise.
- **Measure learning velocity** — interviews, waitlists, revenue experiments beat vanity commits.
- **Protect energy** — transformation is a marathon; burnout is not a badge.
